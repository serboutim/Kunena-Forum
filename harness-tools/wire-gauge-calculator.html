Ampacity is based on single conductors in free air at 30°C, adjusted for insulation rating with √((Tins−Tamb)/(105−30)). Bundle factors follow the NEC 310.15(C)(1) adjustment table. Resistance is calculated at the insulation temperature, which is conservative. Treat these results as engineering estimates and check them against the governing standard (SAE AS50881, ABYC E-11, ISO 6722, NEC) and the wire maker's datasheet.
