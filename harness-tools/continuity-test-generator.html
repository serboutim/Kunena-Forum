TPCavityNetTester pin

DIY tester sketch (Arduino)

Wire each test point to its tester pin through a mating connector on the fixture. The sketch drives one point LOW at a time while the others sit on internal pull-ups, then reports OPEN, SHORT and PASS or FAIL over serial at 115200 baud. This is a low-voltage continuity check only, so use a hipot tester for insulation resistance.
