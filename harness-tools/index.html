Harness Tools

Single-file browser tools for people who design, build and test wire harnesses. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded: your data stays in your browser.

Design calculators

AWG
Wire Gauge & Voltage DropSize wire by drop and ampacity with temperature and bundle derating. AWG or mm².
⌀
Bundle Diameter & Covering SizerBundle OD from your wire list, plus sleeve, loom, heat shrink and grommet sizes.
RD/BK
Wire Color CodesAC, DC, ABYC marine, Ethernet, USB, thermocouple, IEC 60757 and a stripe marker builder.
12V
DC System PlannerVan, marine, motorsport, EV and drone presets, giving wire, fuse, battery bank and main feeder sizes.

Parts & compliance

DT06
Connector Complete-Kit FinderFrom a housing to the mating half, terminals, wedge locks, plugs and tools, with distributor links.
UL
UL / SAE / MIL Wire Style CheckerFind UL AWM, J1128, ISO 6722 or M22759 types for a given voltage, temperature and environment.
BOM
BOM Health CheckScore each BOM line for lifecycle, single source, stock, lead time and RoHS/REACH risk.

Data & revisions

.net
Netlist → Wire ListConvert KiCad or net/ref/pin CSV into a wire list, cut list, WireViz YAML or JSON.
A→B
Revision Diff & ECOSee added, removed and changed wires between revisions, plus a connector rework list.
W12
Wire Label GeneratorPrint-ready marker sheets with destination labels, wrap-around repeats and Avery presets.

Manufacturing & test

$
Quote & Cost EstimatorLabor standards, materials and quantity breaks, producing a branded printable quote.
1-2-3
Work-Instruction GeneratorStep-by-step operator mode with a cavity map, sign-off and traceability log.
Cpk
Crimp Quality LogCrimp height and pull tests against limits, with Cpk and a run chart.
TP
Continuity Test GeneratorNets, a test-point map, a DIY Arduino tester sketch and an open/short checker.
These are engineering aids. Verify the results against the governing standards and the manufacturer datasheets.
